# Figures

The figures of the first table are generated by `python validation/make_figures.py`, the phase diagrams of the second
table by `python validation/make_phase_diagrams.py`, from the results listed with each figure. No data are edited
before plotting. Units: masses and field values in GeV, radii in GeV$^{-1}$; actions are dimensionless.

Terms used in the captions:

* $q=|\lambda_{\min}|/\lambda''_{\min}$, with $\lambda''=d^2\lambda/d(\ln\phi)^2$ at the minimum;
* $\delta=M_t-M_t^{\rm crit}$;
* *Planck-sensitive* means the bounce centre exceeds $M_{\rm Pl}$, or the minimum of $\lambda$ lies at or near
  $M_{\rm Pl}$, so the result depends on freezing the couplings above $M_{\rm Pl}$.

## Phase diagrams (`validation/make_phase_diagrams.py`)

Common to all panels: colours give the status after the shooting fallback (green stability, yellow meta-stability, red
instability, dark grey non-perturbative, light grey Planck-scale dominated with no finite bounce, purple unresolved,
white invalid input $y_t(M_t)\le0$). Hatched yellow: no converged bounce, but metastable by the rigorous bound
$S\ge8\pi^2/(3|\lambda_{\min}|)$, which alone gives $\wp<1$ for every bounce scale up to $M_{\rm Pl}$ (interior
$\lambda$ minimum only). Black lines: $\lambda_{\min}=0$ and $\wp=1$ from the numerical bounce with $\Lambda_B=1/R$.
Star and grey ellipses: PDG 2026 masses with their joint $1\sigma$ and $2\sigma$ regions ($\Delta\chi^2=2.30$, $6.18$).
Prescription `cw1`, extended matching; away from the controlled region $[120,130]\times[166,182]$ GeV the matching is
extrapolated, so those areas are not physical predictions. The two scans are not committed (36 and 55 MB); they
are regenerated with `python python/smvac/run_scan.py configs/full_plane.toml` (or `configs/closeup.toml`)
followed by `python validation/fallback_shooting.py <scan> --matching extended` (README §8). Numbers quoted below
are in `results/phase_diagrams_summary.json`.
